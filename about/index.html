About

A personal photography collection exploring places, people, nature, and moments from my travels around the world.

Hi there,

Welcome to my photography website!

Here you’ll find a collection of photographs capturing the places I’ve explored and the people I’ve encountered throughout my travels.

Photography allows me to capture what it feels like to experience somewhere new, from its atmosphere and character to the people who bring it to life and the details that make it memorable.

I’m particularly interested in the relationship between people and their surroundings: how places shape our emotions and perceptions, how people bring their own stories and emotions into these spaces, and how these interactions create something unique.

As a scientist, I also love using photography to document life over time. I’m curious about how society evolves and fascinated by the complexity of nature at every scale. I enjoy noticing patterns and connections, stepping back to see the bigger picture, and discovering just how much a simple photograph can reveal.

Together, these photographs form a collection of memories gathered over the years and around the world. The places I’ve been, the people I’ve met, and the moments that make me smile.
