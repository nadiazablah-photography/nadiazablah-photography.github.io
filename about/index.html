About

A personal photography collection exploring places, people, nature, and moments from my travels around the world.

Hi there,

Welcome to my photography website. Here you’ll find a collection of places, people, and moments I wanted to remember from my travels.

What I love about photography is the way it allows me to crystallize the feeling of discovering somewhere new, from its atmosphere and character to the details that make it memorable.

I particularly enjoy exploring the relationship between people and their surroundings. I focus on how places can shape people’s emotions and perceptions, but also how people bring their own emotions and stories into the places they inhabit. I try to convey something of that relationship, as well as the sensations I feel while exploring.

I also see photography as a way to document life and observe how it changes over time, which appeals to my scientific mind. I’m curious about how society evolves and fascinated by the complexity of nature at every scale. I like noticing patterns and connections, stepping back to see the bigger picture, and discovering how much a simple photograph can reveal.

This website is therefore a collection of my memories, gathered over the years and around the world, and a reflection of the way I see and experience them.
